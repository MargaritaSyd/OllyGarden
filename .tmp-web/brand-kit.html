Logo

One mark, on every ground

The OllyGarden logo represents the balance between nature and technology. It combines a geometric symbol, a large oval with a smaller circle inside, with a clean, modern wordmark set in Space Grotesk Bold.

  • Full logo with wordmark. The standard version, for most applications.
  • Symbol only. For small-scale or limited-space uses, keeping it recognisable.
  • Gradient version. Adds depth and dimension while keeping proportions and structure the same.
  • Flat, full-colour version. For simpler applications, or wherever a gradient does not suit.

Construction

One large oval and one smaller circle. The inner circle aligns to the left, and the centre of the large oval lines up with the white space of the small circle.

Gradient

The large oval fades from 0% to 100% transparency at 45°, the small circle from 0% to 50% at 60°. Small adjustments that add depth and keep the harmony.

Grid and clear space

A modular grid aligns the symbol and the wordmark at any size. Around the logo, keep clear space equal to the diameter of the inner circle.

Pixel pattern

Type a word, get a pattern

The pixel pattern is one of the key elements of the brand: it pairs with the bitmap imagery and carries the tech side of the look. It can also spell numbers, words and short phrases, handy for simple social assets. Click any tile to change its colour.

01

8 or 16 tiles on the short side

Size the pattern to each asset: put 8 or 16 square tiles along its short side, then repeat them across the whole asset with no gaps or overlaps.

02

About 75% in one colour

Colour each tile from the brand palette, keeping nearly three in four of them the same. With a partner, use their main colour for a few tiles.

03

Text sits in solid zones

Put text on solid colour, align text and images to the tile edges, and use the palette for maximum contrast.

Bitmap imagery

Dither any photo into the garden

Any image can become an OllyGarden asset once it is bitmapped in the green palette. The result should not look like a realistic photograph, nor messy: the dithering is obvious and every major detail stays. Drag across the photo to compare, or try your own. It is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

OriginalBitmap
HighlightsMidtonesShadows
01

Five or six greens

Dither with the green brand palette, matching each shade's brightness to the lights and shadows of the image.

02

Floyd–Steinberg, factor 70–90

Floyd–Steinberg is the recommended method. A higher factor looks noisy and flat; a lower one adds contrast and hides small details.

03

Size 40–50

Size sets the level of detail: the bigger the asset, the higher the number. Too high on a small asset and the bitmap effect disappears.
